Look out for your fellow fans

The member safety center.

Clear boundaries and simple ways to get help make fandom more welcoming. The reporting actions below are previews until moderation is connected.

🛡️

Keep your account safe

Use a unique password, never share verification codes, and avoid posting your home address, school, live location, or private contact details.

💗

Respect fan boundaries

No harassment, hate, doxxing, impersonation, stalking, scam links, or sharing private images and messages without permission. Respect artists’ private lives too.

📣

Report a concern

If a post or interaction feels unsafe, save only the details needed to explain the issue and contact the moderation team. Don’t retaliate or repost harmful content.

What a real safety system should include

  • ✓Report and block controls available from profiles, posts, and messages.
  • ✓A private moderator queue with restricted access, audit logs, and clear response targets.
  • ✓Anti-spam protections, appeal steps, age-appropriate safeguards, and an escalation plan for urgent threats.
  • ✓Data minimization and a clear retention period for reports and evidence.

Important: These are guidance and interface previews only. This static website cannot investigate reports, block members, or provide emergency response. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted person.

Suggested technical setup

Choose services based on budget, region, moderation needs, and the amount of personal data you truly need.

  • 01Frontend
    Keep this responsive HTML/CSS/JavaScript prototype as a visual starting point.
  • 02Authentication & database
    Use a reputable managed service with secure rules, least-privilege access, backups, and deletion workflows.
  • 03Moderation & support
    Route reports to a restricted staff queue; never expose report contents in public pages or analytics.
  • 04Commerce & email
    Use official affiliate links and a consent-aware email provider. Verify program terms before publishing links.
  • 05Launch gates
    Test account security, mobile accessibility, privacy controls, recovery flows, and incident handling before inviting members.

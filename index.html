From idea to launch

Important steps before opening the doors.

The backend starter is in the code; owner-side setup, review, and real-world testing are still required.

01

Identity & account security

Configure Supabase Auth, email verification, password recovery, secure server-side account deletion, and trusted moderator roles. Never store raw passwords.

02

Real chat & groups

The shared group/chat starter uses Realtime and membership policies. Test with two accounts; private invites, block/mute, and notifications remain to be built.

03

Posts & moderation

Shared posts, reporting, and a moderator queue are included in the starter. Media uploads are disabled until storage policies and abuse controls are reviewed.

04

Privacy & launch checks

Customize and review the draft policies, confirm age requirements, test mobile/accessibility, configure backups and monitoring, and verify every RLS rule before launch.

BLOOM Safety Center 🛡️

Your comfort matters here. Keep your account secure, protect your personal information, and help us keep the fandom kind.

🔐

Protect your account

Use a unique password, verify the email address you control, and never share passwords, sign-in links, or verification codes—even with someone claiming to be staff.

💗

Keep personal details private

Avoid posting your home address, school, phone number, live location, travel plans, or private conversations. Ask permission before sharing another person's content.

🚩

Report and get support

If a post or interaction feels unsafe, stop engaging, save only what you need to explain the concern, and tell a trusted adult when appropriate. This preview report form does not send a real report yet.

🤝

Respect the community

No harassment, threats, impersonation, doxxing, scams, or pressure to share private information. Moderation tools must be configured and staffed before a public launch.

Important: BLOOM's report form is currently a preview and is not monitored. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted adult; don't rely on this website to get urgent help.

Privacy notice 🔒

Draft for review — not legal advice. Update this notice to match your actual business, services, jurisdiction, vendors, and data practices before launch.

  • Information: Explain what account details, profile content, posts, messages, and newsletter signup data you collect, and why.
  • Use: Describe how information supports accounts, community features, security, moderation, and requested email updates.
  • Sharing: Identify service providers and any sharing that occurs. Do not sell or disclose personal information beyond what your real practices and applicable law permit.
  • Controls: Explain how members can update or delete profiles, manage email preferences, request account deletion, and contact the site operator.
  • Security & retention: Describe safeguards and how long information is retained, without promising absolute security.
  • Minors: State age requirements and protections clearly. If teens can use the service, design age-appropriate privacy and moderation protections and comply with applicable law.
  • Cookies & affiliates: Disclose analytics, cookies, and affiliate tracking where used.
  • Contact & updates: Add the responsible operator’s contact details, effective date, and a way to learn about material changes.

Before launch, get the final policy reviewed for applicable privacy laws, including Malaysian requirements if relevant.

Community guidelines ♡

BLOOM is a fan-first space. Keep it welcoming, creative, and safe.

  • Be kind: No bullying, hate, threats, harassment, dogpiling, or fan-war baiting.
  • Respect privacy: Never post someone’s private details, location, private messages, or images without permission. No doxxing or tracking idols’ private lives.
  • Respect boundaries: Support idols as people. Use official public sources and respect personal space.
  • Post responsibly: Label spoilers, credit fan creators, respect copyright, and share media only when you have permission.
  • Keep groups safe: No scams, impersonation, sexual exploitation, dangerous challenges, or requests for passwords and verification codes.
  • Trade fairly: Be honest about merch and trades. Do not misrepresent products or use affiliate links deceptively.
  • Report concerns: Use reporting tools when available and contact moderators for urgent community issues. Do not retaliate against reporters.
  • Moderation: Explain how content may be removed and accounts warned, suspended, or banned for violations, with an appeal route where practical.

These are starter guidelines. Add a working report/block system, moderation process, and clear age policy before opening community features.
